Heart Attack First Aid

Short steps you can scan quickly in an emergency.

🚑
Call 911 immediately if you suspect a heart attack.
Emergency responders can start treatment right away.

1) Call 911

Call right away if someone has chest pressure/pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, or sudden collapse.

Emergency

2) Aspirin (only if recommended)

If emergency services or a healthcare professional advises it, the person may chew and swallow aspirin.

Show safety notes

Do not give aspirin if allergic, bleeding risk, or told not to take it.

Only if recommended

3) Nitroglycerin (prescription only)

Help them take it only if it is their own prescribed medication.

Show safety notes

Do not give nitroglycerin to someone who was not prescribed it.

Prescription

4) CPR if not breathing normally

If unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR: push hard and fast in the center of the chest.

Start immediately

5) Use an AED if available

Turn it on and follow voice instructions. Use ASAP.

Use ASAP

Safety note: This is educational only. If you think someone is having a heart attack, call emergency services immediately.
