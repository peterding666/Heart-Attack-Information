Prevention

Simple habits that reduce the risk of coronary artery disease and heart attacks.

Top habits (biggest impact)

  • Don’t smoke or vape
  • Move weekly (aim: ~2 hours/week)
  • Heart-healthy diet
  • Healthy weight + sleep
  • Manage stress
Lifestyle

Medical prevention

  • Check blood pressure
  • Check cholesterol
  • Screen for diabetes
  • Follow up on abnormal rhythms
