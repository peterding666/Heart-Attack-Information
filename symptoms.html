Common Symptoms

Start with the quick-scan signs below. Expand details only if you need them.

⚠️
Call 911 if chest pressure/pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, or multiple symptoms happen together.
When in doubt, treat it as an emergency.

Emergency signs (act now)

  • Chest pressure/pain/tightness
  • Shortness of breath at rest
  • Fainting / near-fainting
  • Pain spreading to arm/jaw/back
  • Cold sweat + weakness
Call 911

See a doctor soon

  • Unusual fatigue or weakness
  • Dizziness / lightheadedness
  • Nausea / vomiting (with other symptoms)
  • Rapid/irregular heartbeat
  • Symptoms with exertion

Chest pain / pressure

Often described as heaviness, squeezing, or fullness.

Show more

It may last minutes or come and go. Many people feel it in the middle or left chest.

Emergency
↗️

Pain spreading

Arm, shoulder, back, neck, jaw, teeth, or upper abdomen.

Show more

Heart-related pain can move beyond the chest and may feel aching or heavy.

Emergency
💨

Shortness of breath

Breathless even at rest or with small effort.

Show more

This can mean the heart is struggling to pump enough blood.

Emergency if sudden/severe
🥶

Cold sweat

Sudden clammy sweating without heat or exercise.

Show more

Can be a stress response from the body during heart strain.

See a doctor soon
😵

Dizziness / fainting

Feeling unsteady, faint, or briefly passing out.

Show more

May happen if the brain isn’t getting enough blood/oxygen. Treat fainting as serious.

Emergency if fainting
🤢

Nausea / indigestion-like pain

Sometimes mistaken for heartburn.

Show more

If unusual for you or paired with chest pressure, sweating, or weakness, take it seriously.

See a doctor soon
